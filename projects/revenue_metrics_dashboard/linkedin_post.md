## 🇬🇧 In English

**$8,440 in MRR — and just $15 of net growth.**

New project: Revenue Metrics Dashboard, built in PostgreSQL + Tableau Public.

The question it answers: are we growing — or is new revenue just covering up what we lose?

Total MRR rose all through 2022. But in December the gains added +$4,342 while contraction and churn removed −$4,328. Net New MRR: **$15**. A top-line revenue chart will never show you that.

→ It's a retention problem, not an acquisition one: User Churn Rate 25.6% → 34%
→ LTV: $236 (July) → $131 (December)
→ Customers aren't leaving — they're downgrading

Main takeaway: a dashboard that's correct at full range is not yet correct. My churn metrics looked right and returned NULL the moment a single month was selected. Test the interaction, not just the numbers.
